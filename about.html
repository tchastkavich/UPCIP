What is The UPCIP?

The Unified Planetary Council of Intergalactic Peace, or UPCIP for short, is exactly what it sounds like,
an alliance between multiple planets all across the universe with a singluar goal, peace across the universe.
The UPCIP was founded in 20XX by [NAME NOT TRANSLATABLE], who wanted to make the galaxy
a safer place for all, without war or conquering other planets or people, a value we still uphold to this day.

Our Mission

We at The UPCIP have made it our mission to solve the problem that is peace among worlds, our ultimate goal
is to create a peaceful galaxy that is safe for all beings from all planets across the universe.
At The UPCIP we believe the path to peace is through peaceful means, which is why we have an incredibly strict
no violence rule, our operations to ensure peace in our time and beyond follow our very strict
policy against violence. The no violence rule may be broken under two situations: 1. A planet or group of planets
is attacking The UPCIP or one of its planets, and refuse to resolve things peacefully,
or 2. Intergalactic conflict is brewing and there is no possible non-violent solution.

What do we do?

In our efforts for galactic peace, we send L.I.F.E teams on special missions to ensure the planets in The UPCIP
have enough resources, are without interplanetary conflict, and follow our guidelines to ensure peace among worlds.
Additionally, there are units known as M.U.R.D.R's, which are soldiers we have in case of war between worlds.
We also have a team that goes from planet to planet promoting The UPCIP and trying to get
more planets to join our peacekeeping efforts.
